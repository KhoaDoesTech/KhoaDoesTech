## <img src="https://media2.giphy.com/media/QssGEmpkyEOhBCb7e1/giphy.gif?cid=ecf05e47a0n3gi1bfqntqmob8g9aid1oyj2wr3ds3mg700bl&rid=giphy.gif" width="28"> About Me

I’m a backend-focused full-stack developer based in Ho Chi Minh City, working with Node.js, TypeScript, Go, and React. At FPT Software, I was one of three engineers who delivered six of thirteen microservices for an enterprise franchise platform. My work covered secure identity, customer and loyalty services, reporting, media uploads, and a Gemini/LangGraph shopping assistant; the platform exposed 217 REST endpoints behind Kong.

I’ve also built Go services for an insurance platform at GooPay and led a three-person team on an e-commerce product. I’m looking for a backend role where correctness, reliable data flows, and clear service boundaries matter.
